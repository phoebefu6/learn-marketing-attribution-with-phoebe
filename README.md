# Learn Marketing Attribution with Phoebe

Who gets the sale? A two-track, interactive course on marketing attribution modeling - from
classic first-touch and last-touch through Markov, Shapley, ML and deep-learning attribution,
marketing mix modeling, and causal geo-lift tests. Built for 2026, honest about the cookieless
reality that broke multi-touch and brought MMM back.

**Live:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-marketing-attribution-with-phoebe/

## Two tracks, one running brand

Every session works on **Lumen Skincare** - a synthetic $18M direct-to-consumer skincare brand
with nine channels and a $4M media budget its CMO needs to defend.

- **Leader track (6 x 45 min, no code)** - for CMOs, CDAIOs, and boards. Read the models, trust
  the right number, and reallocate a budget without getting fooled by a last-click dashboard.
- **Builder track (10 x 45 min, SQL + Python)** - for DA/DE/DS/growth engineers. Build the
  journey table in SQL, then implement heuristics, Markov, Shapley, ML/DL attribution, GA4 DDA
  reconciliation, MMM, and geo-lift tests from scratch on the same data.

## Built from

The leading attribution material - LinkedIn Learning's attribution and mix modeling course, the
Udemy measurement and MMM courses, PyMC Labs' Bayesian marketing analytics, Meta Blueprint's
conversion-lift path, Google's GA4 and Meridian docs, and the key papers (Shao & Li, DNAMTA,
DeepMTA, Shapley). Certificates, videos, and quizzes stay with their official sources.

No build step - static HTML, CSS, and JS. Open `index.html` or serve the folder.
